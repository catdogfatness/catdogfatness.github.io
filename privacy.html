Privacy Policy for Phone Projector

Last updated: June 22, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Phone Projector ("the app") handles your information.

Information We Collect

Phone Projector does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

Camera: The app uses your device's camera to display a live view for tracing purposes. Camera footage is never recorded, saved, or sent anywhere.

Photo Library: The app accesses your photo library only when you choose to select a reference image. Selected images are used locally on your device and are never uploaded or shared.

Advertising

Phone Projector displays ads provided by Google AdMob. The app is configured to serve non-personalized ads only. Google AdMob may collect certain device information to serve ads and for fraud prevention. For more information on how Google handles data, please review Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Third-Party Services

The only third-party service used by this app is Google AdMob. We do not use any analytics, crash reporting, or other tracking services.

Children's Privacy

Phone Projector does not knowingly collect any information from children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
